# Minecraft Nature Terraformer

## Overview

This skill guides subterranean organic landscaping, cave naturalization, and botanical micro-biomes in Minecraft 1.21. It handles terrain blending, lush vegetation, water dynamics, and living ecosystems built inside underground chambers.

## Core Landscaping Principles

### 1. Organic Terrain Blending
Never leave underground garden floors as flat rectangles.
- Natural Contours: Layer moss blocks, grass blocks, rooted dirt, and coarse dirt in irregular clusters.
- Vertical Terracing: Step elevations up by 1 block using moss carpets, stone slabs, and mossy cobblestone retaining walls.
- Path Wear: Transition from primary stone walkways to packed mud, muddy mangrove roots, and coarse dirt near water banks.

### 2. Ceiling Foliage and Hanging Atmosphere
Underground caverns feel vast and ancient when ceilings are actively colonized by vegetation:
- Spore Blossoms: Suspend `minecraft:spore_blossom` from ceiling beams to generate drifting green petal particle effects throughout the chamber.
- Cave Vines: Drop `minecraft:cave_vines[berries=true,age=24]` down 2 to 4 blocks from ceiling vaults to create natural amber glow.
- Hanging Roots and Chains: Interlace iron chains with hanging roots (`minecraft:hanging_roots`) over water channels.

### 3. Aquascaping and Water Features
- Natural Water Channels: Dig 1 block deep riverbeds lined with smooth stone slabs, gravel, and sea pickles for underwater illumination.
- Lily Pads and Flora: Float lily pads on calm pools, flanked by flowering azalea bushes, pink petals, and big dripleaf plants.
- Sunken Aquariums: Enclose aquatic habitats behind tinted glass, populated with sea lanterns, seagrass, tall seagrass, and axolotls or tropical fish.

### 4. Custom Subterranean Trees
Standard oak saplings often fail in enclosed chambers. Construct custom trees:
- Trunk Architecture: 1 to 2 block thick trunks of stripped dark oak or cherry wood, branching outward at 45 degree angles using matching wood stairs and slabs.
- Canopy Volume: Layer flowering azalea leaves, azalea leaves, or cherry leaves into rounded cloud canopies.
- Root Clusters: Extend mangrove roots, rooted dirt, and moss blocks into the surrounding floor to anchor the tree visually.

### 5. Geode and Crystal Formations
For arcane and mystic sectors:
- Cavity Liners: Frame geode alcoves with smooth basalt and calcite.
- Amethyst Clusters: Place small, medium, and large amethyst buds, culminating in fully grown amethyst clusters (`minecraft:amethyst_cluster[facing=up]`).
- Audio Atmosphere: Players walking across calcite and amethyst generate authentic chime notes.
