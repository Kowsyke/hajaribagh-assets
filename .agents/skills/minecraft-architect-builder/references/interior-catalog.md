# Interior Catalog and Furniture Designs

This catalog provides modular furniture and workstation designs using vanilla Minecraft blocks.

## Seating and Tables

### Royal High Table Dais
- Table Top: Spruce slabs elevated on polished andesite pillars.
- Table Runner: Crimson carpet with gold pressure plates at place settings.
- Feast Vessels: Flower pots with dried kelp (as cups) and sea pickles (as goblets).
- Sovereign Throne: Polished deepslate stairs with dark oak trapdoor armrests and a banner backrest.

### Council Round Table
- Centerpiece: Circular table of dark oak trapdoors resting over redstone lamps or sea lanterns.
- Parliament Benches: Horseshoe arrangement of spruce stairs facing inward toward the rostrum.
- Microphones / Rostrum: Lectern placed at the southern head with a daylight detector or skull accent.

## Arcane and Scholarly Furniture

### Grand Athenaeum Book Stacks
- Lower tier: Regular bookshelves alternating with chiseled bookshelves containing enchanted books.
- Upper tier: Mezzanine walkway of spruce trapdoors supported by hanging chains.
- Rolling Ladders: Dark oak ladders mounted against stripped dark oak pillars.
- Study Desks: Polished basalt bases topped with smooth quartz stairs and open books on lecterns.

### Enchanting Sanctum (Level 30 Guaranteed)
- Layout: 15 bookshelves placed in a 5 by 5 perimeter with 1 block air gap around the central enchanting table.
- Accent: Amethyst clusters placed along the floor trim for ambient chime sounds.
- Alchemical Counter: Brewing stand on a cauldron with water, surrounded by potion chests and brewing ingredients.

## Storage and Armory Modules

### Hobbit Pantry Storage Alcove
- Width: 3 blocks wide, 2 blocks deep, 3 blocks high.
- Lower Layer: 1 double chest facing outward (`type=left` and `type=right`).
- Middle Layer: 2 barrels facing outward with an item frame or sign indicator.
- Upper Layer: Upside down spruce stairs forming an arched frame with a hanging lantern.

### Armory Vault
- Weapon Racks: Armor stands equipped with enchanted netherite or diamond armor.
- Wall Mounts: Grindstone and smithing table embedded in polished deepslate wall recesses.
- Security Gates: Iron bars flanked by stone brick walls and tripwire hooks.

## Hearth and Fireplaces

### Roaring Grand Hearth
- Firebed: Netherrack or campfires placed over hay bales (hay bales multiply smoke height threefold).
- Mantle: Polished blackstone stairs and chiseled stone bricks.
- Flue Chimney: Hollow brick column venting upward to surface or exhaust shaft.
- Safety: Surrounding floor must use nonflammable masonry (cobblestone, polished granite, deepslate) to prevent wood fire spread.
