# Architectural Palette Matrix

This matrix defines tested block combinations for subterranean and aboveground builds in Minecraft 1.21.

## Subterranean Hobbit and Dwarven

- Primary Masonry: Stone bricks, cracked stone bricks, tuff bricks.
- Frame and Pillars: Dark oak logs, stripped dark oak logs, polished deepslate pillars.
- Carpentry and Floors: Spruce planks, spruce slabs, stripped spruce wood.
- Accent and Trims: Spruce stairs, dark oak trapdoors, copper grates.
- Emissive Lighting: Lanterns hanging from chains, soul lanterns, copper bulbs.
- Organic Details: Azalea leaves, moss blocks, spore blossoms, glow lichen.

## Arcane Sanctum and Athenaeum

- Primary Masonry: Polished deepslate, deepslate tiles, chiseled tuff.
- Frame and Archways: Nether brick pillars, polished basalt, crying obsidian accents.
- Carpentry and Shelving: Dark oak bookshelves, chiseled bookshelves, warped wood trim.
- Floors: Polished blackstone bricks with inlaid amethyst blocks or gilded blackstone.
- Emissive Lighting: Sea lanterns beneath tinted glass, end rods, enchanting table runes.
- Artifacts: Lecterns, brewing stands, cauldrons, dragon heads, hanging bells.

## Grand Sovereign Council and Feasting Hall

- Primary Masonry: Mud bricks, stone bricks, polished granite skirting.
- Frame and Beams: Mangrove wood, stripped dark oak logs, polished andesite pillars.
- Floors: Checkerboard of polished diorite and dark oak planks, covered by crimson or honey carpets.
- Seating and Tables: Polished deepslate stairs as thrones, spruce slabs for long banquet tables.
- Hearth and Warmth: Dual brick fireplaces with campfires on hay bales for high chimney smoke.
- Emissive Lighting: Chandelier rings of dark oak fences, chains, and warm lanterns.

## Subterranean Hyperloop and Transit Station

- Primary Masonry: Smooth quartz blocks, polished diorite, white concrete.
- Platform Edges: Yellow concrete and black concrete alternating stripes (hazard border).
- Track Bed: Polished deepslate tiles with powered rails and activator rails.
- Ceiling Vaults: Inverted quartz stairs with recessed sea lanterns or redstone lamps.
- Automation: Calibrated sculk sensors, copper bulbs as departure status indicators.

## Organic Greenery and Terraced Hydroponics

- Base Terraces: Mud, packed mud, mossy cobblestone retaining walls.
- Vegetation: Flowering azalea, ferns, large ferns, bamboo, hanging glow berries.
- Water Channels: Smooth stone slabs lining running water streams.
- Lighting: Shroomlights concealed underneath water lily pads and azalea clusters.
